Step kinds

  • Static (default) — inline options, or stepOptionsCallback called ONCE on entry; filtered in the browser.
  • search: true — stepOptionsCallback per debounced keystroke (try /deploy → branch).
  • freeText: true — Enter with no match submits the typed text.
  • shouldShow(selections) — skip a step conditionally (try /report: “Inventory” has no time range).

Esc or ⌫ at the start of your answer steps back one step.
